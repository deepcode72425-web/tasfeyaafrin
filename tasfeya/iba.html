DU IBA Preparation

Follow the steps on the notice board, then practise with free books and platforms.

Notice board: how to prepare for DU IBA

1Know where official information lives

  • IBA-DU website (iba-du.edu)Admission notices, BBA/MBA circulars, exam schedules, course outlines, and academic rules.
  • DU Central Admission PortalOnline application, fee payment, admit card, and equivalence certificates for O/A-Level candidates.
  • IBA BBA Alumni DirectoryConnect with alumni, see career tracks, and find networking events.

2Understand the exam

MCQ, 70 to 80 marks
English, Mathematics, and Analytical Ability. Each wrong answer costs 0.25 marks.
Descriptive, 20 to 30 marks
Written English, essays, argument evaluation, or summary writing.
Viva voce
Shortlisted candidates are interviewed on verbal clarity, reasoning, confidence, and general awareness.

3Gather books for each section

English
Barron's SAT (reading and sentence completion), Barron's GRE or Princeton Review Word Smart (vocabulary), CliffsNotes TOEFL or GMAT Review (grammar and sentence correction).
Mathematics
Barron's SAT Math or GMAT Math Review, plus Nova's GRE Math Prep Course or Manhattan Prep GMAT for harder problems and speed.
Analytical Ability
Barron's How to Prepare for the GRE for puzzles (seating, grouping, scheduling). GMAT Critical Reasoning from Manhattan Prep or PowerScore for arguments and assumptions.
Descriptive writing
IBA past question banks, and regular reading of The Daily Star op-eds, The Economist, and Harvard Business Review.

4Practise with question banks and mocks

Past questions
Collections from previous BBA intakes and MBA 50th to 65th intakes are sold at bookshops such as Nilkhet, Dhaka, and shared on Scribd.
Coaching material
Practice sets from centres such as Mentors', Capstones, or Saifur's follow the DU IBA exam pattern.

5Apply, sit the test, and get ready for the viva

Submit your application, pay the fee, and download your admit card on the DU admission portal. O/A-Level candidates also need an equivalence certificate. After the written test, shortlisted candidates move on to the viva voce.

  • Open the DU admission portaladmission.eis.du.ac.bd

6See where IBA can take you

MNCs and FMCG
Management Trainee programs at companies such as Unilever, British American Tobacco, Nestle, Marico, and Reckitt.
Banking, telecom, fintech
Standard Chartered, HSBC, BRAC Bank, IDLC Finance, Grameenphone, Robi, bKash, and Nagad.
Consulting and tech
Management consultancies, the Big Four, and tech startups in product, operations, and growth.
Startups
Case studies and competitions such as Brandwiz and Battle of Minds build founder skills.
Higher education
Postgraduate study at schools such as Harvard, Stanford, INSEAD, LBS, and Oxford, and scholarships like Fulbright, Chevening, and Erasmus Mundus.
